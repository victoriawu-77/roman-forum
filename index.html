Plan your visit

The Roman Forum is part of the Colosseum archaeological park. Hours shift with the season — confirm the day you go.

Typical hours

30 Mar – 30 Sep
9:00–19:15
1–26 Oct
9:00–18:30
27 Oct – 29 Mar
8:30–16:30

Closed 1 January and 25 December. Last entry is about an hour before closing.

Getting there

Via della Salara Vecchia, 00186 Rome. Metro line B to Colosseo, then the short walk down into the valley. The Forum entrance is shared with the park ticket.
